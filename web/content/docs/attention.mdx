**Attention** is one list of what needs you, across all your chats: a review waiting for your decision, a blocker, a commitment a bot made, an important change. Your bots keep it, and a coordinating bot sums it up in a short brief.

## Open it

- **Mac:** the Attention button in the toolbar shows while something waits, with how many as its badge. **View › Attention** (⇧⌘A) opens it at any time.
- **Windows and Linux:** the Attention button in the chat's header, beside Running tasks, or Ctrl+Shift+A.
- **Phone:** the Attention button beside Settings in the chat list, while something waits.

Each coordinator's latest brief comes first: the bot, when, and its **Decision**, **Changed**, and **Next** lines. Then the items, urgent first, each with its title, the next step, and whether it is a **Review**, **Blocker**, **Commitment**, or **Change**, with the chats it came from.

Click an item to open its chat. Right-click it (long-press on the phone) to open another chat it came from, or to choose **Mark as Resolved**.

## What puts an item there

- A bot reports something you need to know, such as a decision only you can make, a promise it made, or a change worth hearing about.
- Lorca adds some on its own: work [waiting for your review](/docs/reviews#waiting-for-review), a [task](/docs/tasks) that is blocked or awaiting review, work a bot handed off that came back blocked or failed, and [events](/docs/events) or a [channel](/docs/channels) on hold. These leave the list once the work moves on.

When two bots report the same thing, it is one item. A resolved item doesn't come back; new work is a new item.

## The coordinator

Reports go to a coordinator: a group's owner, or the bot of a direct chat, unless you pick one bot to coordinate everything. The coordinator reads the list, resolves what is finished, and posts a brief in its direct chat when something needs you.

## Notifications

The **⋯** menu in Attention has two switches, both on to start:

- **Briefs**: a notification when a coordinator posts a brief.
- **Urgent Items**: a notification right away for an urgent item, from the chat it is about.

The same menu picks the **Coordinator**: **Automatic**, or one bot. A bot's closing reply after a brief stays quiet, so you hear about a thing once. A [muted chat](/docs/chats#organize-chats) sends no notifications, but what it raises still shows in Attention.
