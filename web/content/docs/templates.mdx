Any bot can be shared as a **template**: its profile and the skills, routines, memories, and plugins you pick. Whoever opens it makes a new bot of their own from it, on their own computer, with their own AI provider and sign-ins.

## What a template holds

| Part | What goes in |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | The name, the description (the bot's instructions), its symbol, and its color. Always included. |
| **Skills** | The [skills](/docs/skills) you check. |
| **Routines** | The repeating [routines](/docs/routines) you check, with their schedules and tasks. |
| **Memories** | Lines of the bot's [memory](/docs/memory) and its topic notes. Never its daily log. |
| **Plugins** | Which services the bot works with, such as GitHub. Never your account or sign-in. |

A template never holds your provider or model, the bot's computer or folder, its avatar image, or a sign-in. Anything that looks like a key or a password is removed. Rows that still deserve a look before you share are marked at their end, such as an email address in orange.

## Share it

In the desktop app, open the bot's direct chat and choose **File › Share as Template…**, or right-click the chat in the sidebar. On the phone, tap **Share as Template** in the chat's Details. Check what to include, then:

- **Share Link** makes a link like `https://lorca.app/t/…`, copied for you. The template is encrypted with a key that is part of the link, after the `#`, which browsers never send, so neither lorca.app nor the relay can read it. Anyone you give the link to can.
- **Save as File…** saves a `.lorca-template` file. A file is not encrypted; keep it as private as what is in it.

Sharing the same bot again opens with its link and **Update Link**, which keeps the address.

**Settings › Shared Links** lists your links with their bots and when each was last updated. A link's menu has **Copy Link** and **Revoke Link…**; a revoked link says so to whoever opens it.

## Open a link

A shared link opens a page on lorca.app that shows the bot: its look, name, and description, and its routines, plugins, skills, and memories. **Open in Lorca** opens it in the app, or the page points to the download.

## Make a bot from a template

Choose **File › New Bot from Template…** (on the phone, **New Bot from Template…** in the chat list's New menu), and paste a link or choose a file. Then pick:

- **Name**, the **Runner** it runs on, and the **Provider**.
- For each plugin it works with, which of the Runner's accounts it uses. A plugin the Runner doesn't have yet must be installed and signed in to first; the sheet says what is missing.

**Create Bot** makes the bot and opens its chat. Its routines start paused. The new bot is yours alone: it shares nothing with the bot it came from.
