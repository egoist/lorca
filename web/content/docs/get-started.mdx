title: Get started
description: Install Lorca on your Mac, create your identity, connect an AI provider, and send your first message.

---

You need one AI account: a DeepSeek or Anthropic API key, or a ChatGPT or Grok subscription. This guide uses the desktop app, which runs on macOS 14 or later today. To set up a computer without it, see [Command line](/docs/cli).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Install the app

[Download Lorca for Mac](/download), open the disk image, and drag Lorca to Applications. The app updates itself; **Check for Updates…** is in the Lorca menu.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create your identity

On first launch, choose **Create**. Lorca makes a key pair on this Mac and shows a **backup phrase**: thirteen groups of four characters.

<Callout type="warn" title="Write the phrase down">
  The backup phrase is your identity. It is the only way to restore your account on a new Mac, and nobody can reset it for you. Keep it somewhere safe and offline.
</Callout>

If you already have an account, choose **Restore** and type your phrase, or **Pair** and paste a pairing string from a Mac that is already set up.

</Step>
<Step>

### Name your first bot

Every account starts with one bot, Chef. Keep the name or pick your own.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect a provider

Paste a DeepSeek or Anthropic API key, or sign in to ChatGPT or Grok. The sign-in opens in your browser. You can skip this step and connect later in **Settings › Providers**, but bots cannot answer until one provider is connected.

</Step>
<Step>

### Say hello to Chef

Open Chef's chat and describe your work. Chef proposes a small team and creates it when you agree. Press Return to send; the `+` button attaches files, and the microphone dictates.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What to do next

- [Create a bot yourself](/docs/bots) with **Create New Bot…** in the titlebar.
- [Start a group chat](/docs/group-chats) with two or three bots.
- [Pair your phone or a second computer](/docs/devices). Pairing needs a [relay](/docs/relay).
- [Install a plugin](/docs/plugins) such as GitHub or Linear.
