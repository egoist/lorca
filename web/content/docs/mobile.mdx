The phone app is a full Device on your account: the same keys, the same chats, the same end-to-end encryption. Bots keep running on your computers; the phone talks to them through the relay.

## Install the app

- **iPhone and iPad:** join the beta on TestFlight from the [download page](/download).
- **Android:** download the APK from the [download page](/download) and open it. Android asks you to allow your browser to install apps, and the first time Lorca updates itself, to allow Lorca. The app looks for a new release once a day and offers to install it; **Settings › Updates** checks right away. It needs Android 7.0 or later.

## Pair your phone

In the desktop app, choose **Pair a Device…**, then scan the QR code with the phone app. You can also paste a pairing string from the desktop app or from `lorca pair` on any computer that already has your identity, or open it as a `lorca://pair` link. Pairing needs a [relay](/docs/relay) your phone can reach.

## What you can do

- **Chat** with any bot or group. Replies grow on the phone as the bot writes them.
- **Attach** photos, camera shots, and files.
- **Dictate** messages. Long-press the microphone to change the language.
- **Create bots** for any of your Runners, start group chats, and change a bot's name, description, provider, model, thinking level, [Access](/docs/access), and [limits](/docs/limits) from its Details.
- **Answer cards:** permission cards, plugin sign-ins (the page opens on the phone), [secrets](/docs/secrets) with **Fill In**, a command's question, and a [coding agent](/docs/coding-agents)'s questions.
- **Send drafts:** edit, send, or discard a bot's email and Slack [drafts](/docs/reviews), and approve what waits for review in Details.
- **Follow the work** in a chat's Details: [tasks](/docs/tasks), [outputs](/docs/chats#outputs), a group's [project](/docs/group-chats#the-project), routines with their health, and [channels](/docs/channels). The [Attention](/docs/attention) button in the chat list shows what needs you.
- **Teach bots:** write [skills](/docs/skills) or save one from a chat with **Add Skill › From This Chat…**, give [feedback](/docs/feedback), and accept suggested changes.
- **Share bots:** **Share as Template** in Details, **New Bot from Template…** in the New menu, and **Settings › Shared Links**. See [Share a bot](/docs/templates).
- **Set up [workflows](/docs/workflows)** with **New Workflow** in the New menu.
- **Organize chats** into sections, and hide or mute them. See [Chats](/docs/chats#organize-chats).
- **Manage routines:** pause, resume, run now, or delete from the chat's details.
- **Install plugins** on a Runner, sign in to their accounts, set call limits, manage a bot's [browser profiles](/docs/browser), and edit [Auto-review](/docs/auto-review) rules. Adding a new account is left to a computer.
- **Connect providers** with an API key or subscription sign-in. The credential reaches every paired Device encrypted.
- **Pair and unpair** devices, and update a Runner's command line.

Send another message while a bot works to steer it. Its current model step and tools finish first, then the new instruction joins the same turn. The phone stops one command at a time from **Running tasks**; [Stop](/docs/chats#stop) for a whole turn, and the bot memory editor, are in the desktop app.

## Notifications

When a bot finishes a reply, or asks you something on a card, your phone gets a push with the bot's name and the start of what it said. The content is encrypted on the Runner and opened on your phone. Apple, Google, and the relay see only "New reply".

Tap a notification to open the chat. You get no banner for the chat you have open, nor for one you have open in front of you on a computer, and opening a chat clears its notifications. A [muted chat](/docs/chats#muted-chats) sends none.
