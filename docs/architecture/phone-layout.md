# Phone layout

How the [phone app](phone-app.md) arranges its screens: the tabs a phone opens on, and the two panes of a wide window.

## Home tabs

A phone's home is three tabs in the system's tab bar (`app/(main)/(tabs)/`, expo-router's `NativeTabs`): Bots, the chat list; Devices, the account's Devices (`useDeviceRows` in `src/ui/devices.tsx`), each row sliding in its screen inside the tab's own stack; and Search, a `search`-role tab that iOS 26 sets apart as a round button beside the bar and whose screen (`SearchScreen` in `src/ui/ChatSearch.tsx`) moves its native search field into the bar, while Android's Material bottom bar lists it as a third tab whose screen puts a filled field under the status bar. Each tab is a stack of its own, and a chat opens over the tabs in the `(main)` stack around them, so the bar is gone in a chat. On iOS the Bots tab's navigation bar is that `(main)` stack's (`ChatsBar` in `src/ui/ChatsScreen.tsx`, shown by the tabs layout while Bots is selected), not its own stack's: a chat slides in under the same bar, which fades Settings and New into the chat's back and More buttons in place and keeps its blur, where two bars would slide past each other. Devices and Search keep their stacks' bars. Search lists chats whose title or a member's name matches, then the chats and messages `chats.search` finds (`useChatSearch`, which the sidebar's field uses too).

## Wide windows

A window at least 700 pt wide (a tablet, or a tablet window with room to spare) shows two panes (`app/(main)/_layout.tsx`, `src/ui/layout.tsx`): the chat list as a sidebar (320 pt, 375 pt from 1100 pt) and the open chat beside it; a narrower one stacks the chat over the list. The route group `(main)` holds the tabs and the chat in a stack of their own inside the root stack, so sheets and the pairing screen cover both panes. The sidebar (`src/ui/Sidebar.tsx`) is the same `ChatsScreen` in a native stack of its own, one screen deep, in an independent navigation tree, so it keeps the native bar and toolbar buttons; the tab bar is hidden and Settings lists the Devices; the pane's stack starts at an empty "Select a chat" screen, a chat picked in the sidebar replaces the one open, its row stays lit, and the chat's bar has no back button. Sidebar rows open their menu on a long press on both platforms, with no Link preview. On iOS the sidebar sits in a `CompactWidthView` (`modules/lorca-core`), a container that overrides the horizontal size class to compact as a split view's sidebar column does. On both platforms the sidebar's search field is a capsule at the foot of the list (`src/ui/SidebarSearch.tsx`; glass on iOS, filled on Android), level with the composer and riding the keyboard: UIKit moves a search bar into the bottom toolbar only on an iPhone, and on Android the search action is an item of the activity's one action bar, which react-native-screens hands to whichever stack updated its header last, so a sidebar's item would vanish once the pane opens a chat. The transcript sits in a `SoftScrollEdgeView`, which gives its scroll view the soft top edge a phone has where an iPad would rule a line under the bar. Bubbles and Markdown measure against the pane's width (`usePaneWidth`) and stop growing at 620 pt. The list's sections, its Hidden group, and its Mute and Move to Section menus are in [Sidebar sections and alerts](sidebar.md#in-the-apps).
